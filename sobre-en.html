CYBER
SECURITY

It is a set of practices that protects data and
systems against digital threats, according to
the Law No. 13,709/2018 (LGPD), which ensures
the protection of personal data.

THE PILLARS
OF DIGITAL
PROTECTION

Digital protection is based on
three pillars that help
keep information secure:

CONFIDENTIALITY

Ensures access only for authorized people.

INTEGRITY

Keeps data accurate and secure against unauthorized changes.

AVAILABILITY

Ensures that data is accessible when needed.
