SOCIAL
ENGINEERING

DEFINITION — ✦ ·

It is the use of manipulation techniques to deceive people and obtain information, access, or actions that would not normally be carried out.

HOW IT WORKS

Social engineering works by exploiting people's behavior. First, the criminal identifies an opportunity and chooses an approach that seems natural.

They may use different communication strategies to create a convincing situation and prevent the victim from realizing they are being deceived.

This type of attack is effective because it appears to be something ordinary, causing the person to make decisions without noticing the risk.

PHISHING

One of the most well-known forms of social engineering is phishing. In this type of scam, the criminal pretends to be a trusted person or organization to obtain information or trick the victim into visiting a website, opening a file, or taking an action that may result in a scam.
