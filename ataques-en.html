CYBER ATTACKS

Learn about the main threats in the digital world and the methods used to exploit vulnerabilities in systems and networks.

DEFINITION

Cyberattacks are actions carried out to exploit systems, networks, or devices. They can have different goals, such as disrupting services, accessing data, or causing damage.

VULNERABILITIES

Vulnerabilities are flaws in systems, devices, or user behavior that can be exploited to cause damage or allow unauthorized access.

MAIN TYPES

There are several types of cyber attacks, each with a different way of operating. Among the best known are DDoS, brute-force attacks, vulnerability exploitation, injection attacks, and attacks that use malicious code.

WHO CAN BE AFFECTED

Anyone or any organization that uses the internet can be affected by a cyber attack. Computers, phones, servers, websites, and networks can become targets, regardless of their size or the amount of information they store.

PREVENT THEM

Some precautions can reduce the risk of cyber attacks. Keeping systems updated, using strong passwords, avoiding suspicious content, and enabling security features are important measures. It is also recommended to keep backups of important files.
