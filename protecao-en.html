HOW TO
PROTECT YOURSELF

Small everyday precautions can make a difference in keeping your data, accounts, and devices safer.

PROTECT YOURSELF

Your data is part of your digital life. Protecting it helps prevent unauthorized access, file loss, and account problems.

CHECK

Keep your devices updated, use strong passwords, and enable two-factor authentication. These precautions help protect your accounts and information.

STAY ALERT

Not everything on the internet is what it seems. Before clicking, downloading, or replying, check whether the message and its source are truly trustworthy.

MAKE BACKUPS

Making regular backups allows you to recover information if it is lost or compromised.

DIGITAL PRIVACY

Avoid leaving personal information exposed on the internet. Review your account privacy settings and think before posting data that could be used by other people.
